# Support

- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue on [php-io-extensions/pixman](https://github.com/php-io-extensions/pixman/issues). Include `php --ri pixman`, your OS and CPU, PHP version and build (NTS or ZTS), and the smallest script that shows the problem.
- **How a pixman call behaves:** the comments in [`lib/pixman/pixman/pixman.h`](lib/pixman/pixman/pixman.h) and pixman's sources are the reference; this extension adds only what the README's translation section describes.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.
